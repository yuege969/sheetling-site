Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

14 days, no questions asked. If Sheetling does not do what you needed, ask for your money back. You do not have to explain why, and we will not ask you to prove that it failed.

How to request

Email yuege969dev@gmail.com from any address with either:

  • your Creem receipt / order number, or
  • the license key you were sent (it starts with SL1.).

We reply within two business days and process the refund through Creem to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, the money typically appears within 5–10 business days. Creem's own fees are not deducted from your refund.

What happens to the license

Refunded licenses stop being valid — please remove the extension. There is no penalty and no blacklist; you are free to buy again later at the then-current price.

Why the trial exists

Three complete conversions are free, precisely so that you do not have to buy in order to find out whether it works on your documents. If your documents are scanned images, or you need OCR, the trial will tell you in the first minute — that is the intended way to find out, and a refund is still fine if you bought anyway.

Chargebacks

If something went wrong, please email us before contacting your bank. A chargeback takes weeks and costs us a fee; a refund takes a day and costs nothing.
