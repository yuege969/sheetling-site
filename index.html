What you get

Numbers that calculate

Amounts, dates and percentages are written as real numbers and dates — ready to SUM, sort and pivot. Long IDs (invoice numbers) stay as text so leading zeros survive.

Merged cells from vector lines

Tables drawn with borders are rebuilt from their lines, so merged headers come out as merged cells in Excel, not blank ones.

One sheet per table

Multiple tables in one PDF become multiple sheets, named by page (p1, p2-1, p2-2).

Fixable, not final

Detected tables appear as boxes: drag to move, corner to resize, click to exclude. When the automatic guess is wrong you fix it in seconds instead of re-exporting.

Locale-aware

1.234,56 and 1,234.56 are read as the same amount; 28.10.2024 becomes a real date. Ambiguous formats are left as text rather than guessed.

English and Chinese UI

Follows your browser language; switchable in the top bar. Chinese invoices export with Chinese sheet names.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Text-layer PDFs only. Scanned or photographed PDFs need OCR and are not supported. We would rather say so than sell you a guess.
  • Up to 50 pages / 20 MB per file. Larger or password-protected files are rejected with a clear message.
  • Not a layout clone. Multi-row wrapped cells are merged into one row, and grouped sub-lists are flattened. The output is meant to be calculated with, not pixel-matched to the PDF.

Questions

Does the free trial require an account or a card?

No account, no card, no email. Install the extension and convert three files. The key field only appears when you want more.

How do I know my PDF isn't uploaded?

The manifest requests no permissions and no host permissions, and the extension's own Content-Security-Policy only allows connect-src 'self' — so an outside request is blocked by Chrome, not by our good intentions. Open DevTools → Network, convert a file, and watch it stay empty. The privacy page has the exact steps.

Which files does it work well on?

Invoices (including Chinese e-invoices and English bills), bank statements, expense reports, government statistical yearbooks, and tables inside papers with ruled lines. Results are best when the table has visible column alignment or borders.

What if it gets the box wrong?

Drag it, resize it with the bottom-right corner, or draw a new box anywhere on the page. Only the boxes you keep are exported.
